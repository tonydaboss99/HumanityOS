# HumanityOS Matching Engine (Version 1)

## Goal
Match workers with opportunities based on their skills and availability.

## Matching Process

1. Collect worker profile.
2. Collect business request.
3. Compare worker skills with required skills.
4. Recommend the best match.
5. Notify the worker and the business.

## Future Improvements

- AI-powered matching
- Skill scoring
- Availability ranking
- Location-aware matching
- Learning recommendations
